RetailOS — Business / POS Source Switcher Concepts

6 ways to redesign the top-left business switcher (Daily Centro · Steamy Co. · Publico · SipStop · 7-Eleven) — click any business tab to test

Current Business Switcher (Before)

Plain grey trough with identical text buttons—no brand monograms, live status indicator, or visual distinction for 7-Eleven.

Option 1 · Recommended

Tactile Monogram Chips + Live Sync Dot

Adds a 20px monogram badge (`DC`, `SC`, `PB`, `SS`, `7E`) to each tab: the active business lights up in signature blue with a live green dot, and 7-Eleven sits cleanly after a subtle divider.

Option 2 · Architectural Flush

Flush Topbar Tabs + Sliding Blue Underline

Removes the heavy grey box entirely—businesses sit flush in the header bar with a glowing status dot and a crisp 2.5px blue bottom bar that slides along the header border.

Option 3 · Operational Context

Two-Line POS Engine Deck

Pairs each business name with a micro monospace subtitle (`STOREHUB · LIVE`, `LOYVERSE`, `REPORT`) so you always see which POS source backs each brand.

Option 4 · Tactile Hardware

Mechanical Keycap Bar with Shortcut Index (1–5)

Gives each business a raised physical keycap feel with a numbered index badge (`1`–`5`) for fast scanning and optional keyboard switching.

Option 5 · Unified Instrument Bar

Split `POS` Stamp + Soft Blue Active Pill

Replaces the grey trough with a crisp white bordered bar, a left `POS` status stamp, and the exact soft-blue active pill (`#eff6ff`) used on your Today & Sales filters.
